How I think

A simple operating model.

Three principles I lean on under ambiguity, pressure, and a stakeholder list that won't quit.

01 / OUTCOMES OVER OUTPUTS

Behaviour change beats tickets closed.

I measure success by business and user impact. Before any PRD: what shifts if we build this, and how will we know? Outputs are how I track progress — outcomes are how I keep score.

02 / DATA-INFORMED

Numbers say what. Discovery says why.

Quantitative data shows what; qualitative interviews surface the why. I pair agent and caseworker conversations with A/B rigour across channels — validate before scaling.

03 / STRUCTURED AMBIGUITY

Auditable trade-offs, reversible decisions.

In regulated, complex environments I lean on RICE prioritisation, run alignment workshops across up to 26 stakeholder funds, and document the trade-offs so every decision is auditable and reversible.
