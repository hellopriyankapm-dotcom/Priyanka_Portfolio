— Act I

How I think about product.

Product management is a series of small bets made under uncertainty. The discipline is not in choosing fast — it's in noticing earlier which bets are wrong. I keep teams close to users, evidence honest, and decisions reversible until they have to land. When the work is right, it looks quiet from the outside and inevitable in retrospect.
